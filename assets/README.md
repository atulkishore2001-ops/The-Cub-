# Shared assets for The Cub flyers

Saved from the OSIT Level-1 flyers so they can be reused in the next promotional flyer.

## Logos (`logos/`)

| File | What it is | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `the-cub-stacked.png` | The Cub logo: tiger face above "THE CUB". Original artwork from the Sherlock Squad PDF, transparent background. | **Master version.** 792 px wide, use this for print. |
| `the-cub-horizontal.png` | The horizontal lockup used at the top-left of the OSIT flyer: tiger face on the left, "THE CUB" on the right. | Cut out of the flyer image, so it is only about 344 px wide and the thinnest whisker and mouth lines are faint. Fine for screens, too soft for print. |
| `speechgears.png` | SpeechGears logo with its "Therapeutics for Special Needs" tagline, used at the top-right of the OSIT flyer. Transparent background. | Cut out of the flyer image, about 265 px wide. For print, ask SpeechGears for their original file. |

On the OSIT flyer the two logos sit in the top corners: The Cub on the left, SpeechGears on the right.
