## 4. Picture and sound

- **Framing.** The camera was wide (her face is about 17% of the frame width), so every shot is reframed closer, like the reference: 1.60× for close shots and 1.36× for medium shots, with her face held at the same place in each. The framing only changes at the 7 section cuts, never inside a sentence.
- **Grade.** Light and natural: a little contrast, a touch of warmth, a soft vignette and gentle sharpening after the reframe. The three shots already matched in exposure.
- **Voice.** Each section is levelled to the same loudness, then light noise reduction, EQ, de-esser and gentle compression.
- **Music.** A quiet, warm piano-pad bed that ducks under her voice and resolves on the logo, then fades out like the reference clip. The Sitaara reel has no music under the speech; use the NoMusic version for that look, or to add a trending track inside Instagram.
- **Effects.** Only two, both very quiet: a soft swish when the name card comes in and a chime on the logo.

## 6. For the next shoot

- Frame closer (chest up). The edit had to zoom in 1.4–1.6×, which softens the picture a little.
- The audio is noise-gated, so silences are digital silence. That made the cuts easy here, but 5 seconds of room tone helps when a gate isn't used.
- The question "Do we allow a child to just be a child?" made a great hook. Leading with a line like that on camera saves cutting later.
