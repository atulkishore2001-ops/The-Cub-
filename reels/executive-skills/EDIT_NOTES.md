## 1. Review of the raw video: what can be made better

### What already works
- **Relatable hook.** "The school bus is five minutes away…" is a scene every parent knows.
- **Credibility.** "I'm a developmental psychologist" lands early.
- **A strong reframe.** *"What might look like ignoring… might actually be a child who's struggling to organize themselves."* This is the heart of the video.
- **Actionable tips.** Break the task down, one instruction at a time, use a visual schedule, keep mornings predictable.
- Clean audio, good eye contact, and a variety of backdrops.

### Filming (for the next shoot)
1. **Shoot in 1080p or 4K.** The source is 720p, which limits how far punch-ins can go before the image gets soft.
2. **Framing is inconsistent.** The posters shot is tight, but in the corridor and sofa shots the subject is small with a lot of ceiling and headroom. Aim for a chest-up mid-shot with eyes about one third from the top, and keep the top ~20% clear for on-screen text. (I digitally reframed every location to even this out.)
3. **Colour and lighting change a lot between locations** (green outdoor, cool corridor, warm sofa/plant). Lock white balance and exposure, or use 2–3 locations that each match a section (problem → explanation → solution).
4. **Add B-roll.** Even 4–5 phone clips of about 2s each (shoes by the door, a lost sock, a school bag, a child looking around, a picture/visual schedule on the fridge) would make the hook and tips far more visual.
5. **Use one location per idea.** Location changes worked best where they lined up with a new idea. Plan the shoot so each new backdrop starts a new section.
6. **Keep the energy up in the seated (sofa) section** with a little more hand movement or a closer frame.

## 2. What was done in the edit

### Cut (107s → 59.5s)
- Removed fillers, the no-payoff promise, repeated lines and the long ending (listed above).
- **Tightened pauses.** Any silence over 0.2s inside a sentence was shortened. Every cut lands on a silence, and every splice was checked by re-transcribing it so no word is clipped.
- **Global speed 1.03×** with pitch-preserving time-stretch. This is not noticeable, but it lets the full message fit under 60s.
- **Jump cuts:** zoom alternates between cuts in the same location, so the cuts read as intentional punch-ins.
- **Location changes:** each one has a zoom-blur whip transition, a light flash and a whoosh.
- **Reframing per location** so the face size stays consistent, plus a slow push-in on every shot and emphasis punch-ins on key lines.
- Light colour grade (contrast/saturation), subtle sharpening and a vignette.

**Captions:** word-by-word throughout, with the active word highlighted in yellow and keywords tinted. They sit in the Reels safe area, clear of the bottom caption bar and the right-hand buttons.

### Sound design
- **Voice:** cuts levelled to each other, high-pass, de-mud EQ, presence boost, gentle compression. Final mix at −14 LUFS with true peak at −1.5 dBTP.
- **SFX:** synthesised, so they are 100% royalty-free. Clock tick-tock under the hook, whooshes on transitions, bus horn, pops on bubbles and chips, cartoon "boing" on the socks, a "question" blip, bass hit on ≠, a soft "wrong" buzz on the strike-throughs, ascending dings for each tip, a sparkle on the key titles, and a riser under the growth bar.
- **Music:** a light kalimba/marimba bed, also synthesised and royalty-free. It ducks automatically under speech, the groove starts after the hook, a glockenspiel layer lifts the "what can you do" section, and it ends on a final chord on the end card.
