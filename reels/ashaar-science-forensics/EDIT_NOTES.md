## 2. Review of the raw footage: what could be better

### Works well
- A relatable hook ("Does your child hate doing homework?") followed straight away by a reframe ("you might have a little scientist at home").
- A concrete, visual experiment (bucket, water, everyday objects). It's easy to picture and easy to copy.
- Warm, calm delivery. Eye contact and framing are good. 1080p60 gave room to reframe.
- The clean mic audio made precise cuts possible.

### Could be better
1. **Voice level is very low.** The clips measure −27 to −32 LUFS (Instagram expects about −14), and the "Hi, I am Ashaar…" line is roughly 10 dB quieter than the rest. I boosted each sentence to a common level, but a higher mic gain next time gives a cleaner result.
2. **Dead silence between phrases** (the audio looks noise-gated). It makes cuts clean but sounds unnatural, so the music bed fills those gaps. Record a few seconds of room tone on the next shoot.
3. **Clip 2 ends mid-sentence** ("…read about why things float or sink") and clip 3 starts "Fill a bucket…" with no bridge such as "try this instead". The edit drops "For example," and lets it flow, but a spoken bridge would be better.
4. **Head position drifts** about ±100 px and he leans toward the lens in places. I tracked the face in the edit so the framing stays steady. A tighter, fixed frame helps.
5. **A new backdrop for almost every sentence** (6 in 63s). It adds energy but can feel busy. Fewer locations would make it calmer.
6. **The promo copy** says "starting 14 November" but not the year, venue, age group, price or how to register. Add these to the post caption or pinned comment.
7. **Promo audio**: Audio 1 reads "…starting 14 November, let the children become little detectives" (an imperative); a version such as "…starting 14 November, children become little detectives" may read more naturally. Audio 1 also had 3.3s of silence before the voice and Audio 2 had 1.2s (trimmed).

## 3. What was done

### Cut (89.5s of material → 69.2s)
- Dropped silences and tightened pauses: about 0.14s inside sentences and about 0.1s between sentences (it's a brisk, reel-style pace).
- Removed one filler: "For example," (before "instead of asking your child…").
- Promo pauses longer than 0.42s were shortened to 0.30s. Lead-in silence on Audio 1 and 2 was removed.
- The explainer plays at 1.06x (pitch preserved) to fit the limit; it isn't noticeable.
- All cuts land on silence; each splice was checked by transcribing the finished voice track.

### Layouts
- **Full-screen** speaker for the hook, intro, and the "different way to learn / float or sink / curiosity" lines.
- **Split-screen** (cream panel above, speaker in a rounded window below) for the two idea-heavy sections: "lazy / irresponsible → struggling → the wrong way → ADHD → intelligence & creativity" and "bucket experiment → predict / test / ask / learn". The panel slides open and the picture eases down, so the face always sits below the panel edge.
- Face tracking keeps the face at a consistent height, and zoom is calmer in split-screen mode.
- **Push transitions** (whip with motion blur) on each change of location; jump cuts with alternating zoom otherwise.
- **Promo:** the cream panel expands to cover the frame, so the promo feels like the same visual world as the explainer. Same fonts, same caption style, same colours.

### Sound
- **Voice:** each sentence levelled, high-pass, de-mud and presence EQ, light compression. The promo voice was matched to the explainer by measured loudness (it was 5.8 dB quieter before matching).

- **Music:** a warm, minimal pad/pluck bed, also synthesised. It ducks under speech and lifts slightly in the promo.

## 5. Quality checks done

- **Face safety:** a face detector ran on 684 frames of the explainer (every second frame, skipping mid-push transitions) and checked that no graphic, caption or the cream panel overlaps the face, hair or chin. Final result: 0 violations. This check also caught and fixed real problems along the way (the head leaning into the lens, captions crowding the chin in split-screen, graphics still fading while the panel moved).
- **Timing:** each sentence-opening word was snapped to the true start of the voice (the recogniser's first-word times ran up to 0.3s early on the promo audio). Promo captions and graphics now line up with the voice to within 3 ms.
- **Voice cuts:** the finished voice track was transcribed again to confirm no word was clipped at any splice.
- **Independent review:** three separate reviewers looked at frames of the finished reel (text proofreading; face and layout; promo and branding). They found no spelling errors, no emojis and no face problems, and flagged layout issues that were then fixed: chips running to the frame edge in the bucket and step panels, a clipped handwriting icon, a caption gap during "concentrate", a split "14 | November" caption, an empty panel beat at the start of the split-screen, uneven promo spacing, and a closing lockup that was on screen too briefly (now about 1.1s).
