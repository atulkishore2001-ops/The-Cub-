## 2. Review of the raw footage

### Works well
- **A strong, relatable opening.** Four parent worries in the first 17 seconds.
- **Calm, credible delivery and set.** The desk, books and plant signal "professional", and the clean wall above her leaves room for text.
- **A clear structure:** concerns → what it is → signs → reassurance → what to do.
- **Steady camera, even light and clean audio.** Exposure doesn't drift between clips.

### Could be better next time
1. **The framing is wide** (her face is about 10% of the frame width), so the edit reframes to about 1.2×. Framing from mid-chest up gives a sharper image.
2. **The voice level is low** (−31 to −34 LUFS) and varies about 6 dB between sentences. A higher mic gain helps.
3. **The silence is noise-gated** (dead digital silence between phrases). Recording 5 seconds of room tone gives cleaner edits.
4. **She changes position at two camera cuts in the raw** (around 0:17 and 0:44 in the reel). They are kept as straight cuts in the pause.
5. **"Get an assessment" has no next step** (no handle, link or phone) in the video. Add it to the post caption.

## 5. Things to confirm

- **"aren't bright":** all three speech-recognition models hear "aren't **right**". From the meaning she almost certainly means "bright", so the captions and the card say "bright".
- **"marks are low":** one model hears "new". "Low" fits the meaning and is what the word-level model hears.
- **Wording that differs from the original brief.** The brief asks for "not a reflection of intelligence", "seek an appropriate assessment" and "children aren't lazy; they learn differently". She says "nothing to do with intelligence", "get an assessment" and "your child is not lazy, he just learns differently". The on-screen text uses her words, so text and voice agree.
- **Contact route:** none was supplied (see section 2).
- **Cover image:** `cover_option_1.jpg` ("Struggling in academics?") or `cover_option_2.jpg` ("Your child is not lazy. He just learns differently.").
